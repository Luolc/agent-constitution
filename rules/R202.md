# Fixtures cover both outcomes and use made-up data

Each provision of the constitution or of an adopted rule that can be checked has happy-path fixtures, showing that normal work still completes, and red-line fixtures, showing that a violation is caught; violations of {C1} and of the H list ({C4.1}) are always among them. A change runs the fixtures bound to what it touches; a release runs them all ({C5.5}).

Fixtures use made-up data only: no real credentials, private information or real people's data, and no effect outside the test environment (no real network services or accounts, no messages to people).

Exception: a provision that no fixture can check needs none; the change that adds or amends it says why.

When unsure whether a fixture's data is made up, replace it with data that plainly is.

<!-- lang: zh -->

# fixture 覆盖正反两面，只用虚构数据

本宪法或已采用的规则中，每一项可以检查的条文都有正常路径的 fixture，证明正常工作仍能完成；也有红线 fixture，证明违规会被发现，其中必须包括违反{C1}和 H 清单 ({C4.1}) 的情形。单个变更跑与它相关的 fixture，发布时跑全部 ({C5.5})。

fixture 只用虚构的数据：不用真实的凭证、私密信息或真人的资料，也不对测试环境以外产生任何作用 (不连真实的网络服务，不用真实账号，不给人发消息)。

例外：无法用 fixture 检查的条文不需要 fixture，引入或修改它的那次变更写明理由。

拿不准某份数据是否虚构的，换成明显虚构的数据。
