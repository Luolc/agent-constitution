## Interpretation

- {#C11.1} Sentences with *must*, *never* or *only* are hard rules; the rest are principles applied with judgment. Where the words allow more than one reading, take the one that best serves the provision's purpose; never read a provision so that it grants more authority, or binds less, than its purpose allows.
- {#C11.2} This text borrows the form of a constitution, not legal doctrine: a legal default applies only where this text states it. In particular, neither the more specific nor the newer text prevails by default, since it may exceed its authority or lack adoption; a conflict is resolved only by an explicit change ({C6.3}).
- {#C11.3} The English and Chinese texts are equally authoritative. Where they differ in meaning, the provision is undetermined ({C6.5}) until they agree again; neither text prevails.
- {#C11.4} If a provision cannot apply in a case, for example because the runtime forbids it ({C6.1}), the rest of this text still applies, and so does that provision as far as it can.

<!-- lang: zh -->

## 解释

- {#C11.1} 表达「必须」「不得」或「只能」之意的句子是硬性规则，其余是原则，凭判断适用。条文的字面可以有不止一种理解时，取最符合其目的的那一种；不得把条文解释成授予的权限比其目的允许的更多，或者约束比其目的要求的更少。
- {#C11.2} 本宪法借用的是宪法的形式，不是法律学说：法律上的默认规则，只有本宪法写明的才适用。特别是，「特别法优于一般法」「新法优于旧法」都不适用，因为更具体或更新的文本可能超出了自身的权限，也可能未经采纳；冲突只能通过明文修改来解决 ({C6.3})。
- {#C11.3} 英文本和中文本同等有效。两种文本的意思不一致时，该条文按「未定」处理 ({C6.5})，直到两种文本重新一致；任何一种文本都不优先。
- {#C11.4} 某一条文在个案中无法适用时，例如运行环境禁止 ({C6.1})，本宪法的其余部分照常适用，该条文也在仍能适用的范围内继续适用。
