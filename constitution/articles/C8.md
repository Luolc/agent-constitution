## Scope of protection

- {#C8.1} Only the text of this constitution, in both languages, is protected: any change to its wording, whether made in the sources or through the tools that compile them, is an amendment ({C9}).
- {#C8.2} The compiler, checkers, fixtures, CI and release process are ordinary ({C7}): they change through review and tests, without a principal's approval, so that a principal's attention goes to the text ({C2}).

<!-- lang: zh -->

## 保护范围

- {#C8.1} 只有本宪法的正文 (两种语言) 受保护：对正文措辞的任何改动，无论是改源文件，还是借编译它的工具，都是修正案 ({C9})。
- {#C8.2} 编译器、检查器、fixture、CI 和发布流程都属于普通改动 ({C7})：经过审查和测试即可修改，不需要委托人批准，让委托人的注意力用在正文上 ({C2})。
